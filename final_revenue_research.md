# STARMILK Final 2B2 Revenue Research — Source Notes

**Reference date:** 2026-08-20  
**Scope:** Verified platform capabilities relevant to the existing STARMILK support, membership, and artist-direct paths. These sources do not prove STARMILK-specific conversion or revenue outcomes.

## Official Patreon guidance

Source: [Patreon — Launch Checklist](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002958403-Launch-Checklist-Everything-You-Need-to-Know-Before-You-Hit-Publish)

Patreon’s creator guidance supports a clear, simple membership proposition: a recognizable page, an immediate welcome artifact, and one or two strong benefits before expanding. Patreon allows paid memberships and one-time purchases of posts or collections. The page can surface a curated home layout, custom tabs, shop items, events, collections, or external sites. The platform also documents creator-controlled welcome notes, promotions, and optional special offers.

**STARMILK implication:** The existing single $5/month Patreon message can be made more conversion-ready by naming one immediate, tangible listening artifact and a first-month sequence. This is a recommendation for offer clarity—not an assertion that more tiers or discounts will outperform the current path.

## Official Bandcamp guidance

Source: [Bandcamp — Setting up your artist subscription](https://get.bandcamp.help/en/articles/15263039-setting-up-your-artist-subscription)

Bandcamp documents artist subscriptions, a draft-before-publish editor, and the ability to connect an existing Stripe account for subscription payments. Its current guide recommends a weekly automatic Stripe payout schedule for refund availability.

**STARMILK implication:** If STARMILK has or chooses to establish an artist Bandcamp presence, a subscription can be considered as a music-native recurring path. This is conditional: the audit did not verify a STARMILK Bandcamp catalog or a strategic reason to add another subscription surface alongside Patreon.

## Official Kit capabilities

Source: [Kit](https://kit.com/)

Kit describes creator-facing landing pages, forms, segmented email, visual automations, digital-product commerce, and analytics. The company explicitly identifies musicians among its creator use cases.

**STARMILK implication:** The deployed site has no email input, creating a confirmed owner-controlled audience-capture gap. Any implementation requires a selected provider, consent/privacy copy, and an integration decision; the audit must not add an inert form or collect email without a delivery path.

## Evidence boundary

No current STARMILK traffic, email list, audience cohort, subscriber count, conversion rate, recurring revenue, price test, or product demand data was available to this audit. Therefore, all strategic priorities should be deployed with tracked hypotheses and explicit instrumentation—not treated as forecasted revenue.
